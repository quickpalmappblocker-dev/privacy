QuickPalmApp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

QuickPalmApp helps you block distracting apps and websites. We built it so that your data stays on your phone.

What the app accesses

  • Installed apps: the list of apps on your home screen, so you can choose which ones to block.
  • Accessibility Service: detects which app is open and reads the address bar of supported browsers, only to block the apps and websites you chose (and adult websites if you turn on the 18+ filter).
  • Usage Access: how long you use each app, to show screen time and enforce daily limits.
  • Display over other apps: to show the block screen.

What we collect

Nothing. All of the above is processed on your device only. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. We do not collect, sell or share any personal or sensitive data.

What is stored on your device

Your block lists, schedules, limits and settings. Your password is stored encrypted. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include your settings (not your password) in your own Google account backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. This removes everything the app stored.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
